Questions

Before you buy.

How is this different from just asking ChatGPT or Claude?

On its own, your AI assistant can write a script. It can't make the voice, pictures, captions, thumbnail and finished video, check each step, cut the Shorts and post them. The Kit gives it the tools and the step-by-step method to do all of that on your computer. It also studies the videos already winning your topic before it writes.

Is it a website or an app I log into?

No. It's a download that plugs into Claude or ChatGPT on your Mac or PC. After setup you get your own Studio page on your computer, where you pick looks and voices and see every video.

Do I need any tech or editing skills?

No. Setup takes about 15 minutes, and your AI assistant does it with you. No terminal commands. Every decision comes as a few options with one marked as recommended, so you just pick.

What else will I pay for?

Pictures, clips and voice come from the AI services the Kit has built in (ElevenLabs, Higgsfield, OpenAI (ChatGPT’s image model and voices), Google Veo, fal.ai or Fish Audio), paid direct at their own price, or you can use your own files. You pick the model from a menu with prices. The Kit shows the price before every spend, and autopilot stays under a weekly limit you set. Topic research and stock footage are free. Claude's free plan works. ChatGPT needs a paid plan.

Which kinds of videos can it make?

Long videos and Shorts, in 12 looks: photo-real, 3D cartoon, fantasy, stick figures, archival film, stock footage and more. Captions, music, animated charts and a thumbnail are included. You get a finished MP4. Final Cut, Premiere and DaVinci Resolve timelines are there if you want them.

Will it post for me?

Yes, after you say yes. It uploads to YouTube and posts Shorts to TikTok, Instagram, X, Facebook and Threads. Nothing goes public without your OK.

What if I get stuck?

The Studio has a Help box with instant answers, and it can hand you over to your own AI assistant. If something breaks, one click sends us a bug report, and we tell you when it's fixed.

What if it's not for me?

Ask for a refund within 7 days. No questions. Monthly cancels any time. When a membership ends the Kit stops, but every video you made stays on your computer.

Will my channel make money?

We can't promise that. Nobody honest can. Earnings depend on your niche, how often you post and YouTube's own rules. The Kit makes the videos. Growing the channel is still up to you.
