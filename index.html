Before you buy

Does it upload to YouTube for me?

Not yet. Today you download the finished video and thumbnail, and copy the title, description, tags and pinned comment with one tap each. Publishing to your channel from the app, now or on a schedule, is coming soon.

What does a video cost?

A Short uses about 185 credits and a 10-minute video about 360–425, depending on the look. Starter’s 2,000 credits a month make about 5 ten-minute videos or 10 Shorts. AI-filmed clips cost much more; see what a video costs. You always see the most a video can use before it starts.

How do credits work?

Your plan adds credits to your wallet each month. You’re charged what each video actually used, and the rest stays in your wallet. Unused plan credits roll over to the next month (up to one month’s worth), and top-up credits never expire while you have a plan.

Do I need to install anything, or have my own AI accounts?

No. It all runs in your browser, on your phone or computer. The writing, pictures, voice and footage are included and paid from your credits.

How do I sign in after I buy?

Checkout takes you straight to app.facelessvideokit.com, and we email you the link. Press Sign in with Whop and use the email you bought with: Whop emails you a code, so there’s no password to remember.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full within 7 days of paying, as long as you haven’t started a video yet. Once a video has used credits, or after 7 days, payments aren’t refundable. See the refund policy.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Post them wherever you like. Stock footage comes from Pexels and Pixabay under their free licences. Finished files stay in the app for 7 days, so download what you want to keep.

Can I change the script or thumbnail?

Yes. Ask for up to three script changes per video, and up to three new thumbnails, each priced before it’s made.

What are the Niche Pack and the Launch Bundle?

Optional add-ons, paid once with your first month. The Niche Pack gives you 5 researched niches and 100 topics; pick one and your channel is set up for you. The Launch Bundle adds the Thumbnail & Title Pack: 40 title formulas and 6 thumbnail layouts your videos use automatically. Both get new material every month while your plan is active.

Will my videos get views or make money?

We can’t promise that, and nobody honest can. You get a well-made video on a topic you chose; growing a channel is still up to your topics, how often you post, and YouTube.
