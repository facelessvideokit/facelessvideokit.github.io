Before you buy

How do credits work?

Your plan adds credits to your wallet each month: 2,000 on Starter. Before each video you see the most credits it can use; when it’s done you’re charged what it actually used, and the rest stays in your wallet. The app also shows a weekly estimate for each channel, so you know how far your credits go.

Do I need to install anything, or have my own AI accounts?

No. It all runs in your browser. The writing, pictures, voice and footage are included and paid from your credits.

How do I sign in after I buy?

Go to app.facelessvideokit.com and press Sign in with Whop. Use the same email you bought with: Whop emails you a code, so there’s no password to remember. Checkout also takes you straight there, and we email you the link.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full within 7 days of paying, as long as you haven’t started a video yet. Once a video has used credits, or after 7 days, payments aren’t refundable. See the refund policy.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Download them and post them wherever you like. Stock footage comes from Pexels and Pixabay under their free licences. Finished files stay in the app for 7 days, so download what you want to keep.

Can I change the script or thumbnail?

Yes. Ask for up to three script changes per video, and up to three new thumbnails, each priced before it’s made.

How long does a video take?

You’ll see a live progress bar with a time estimate. You don’t need to wait on the page: close the tab and we’ll email you when it’s ready, or when it needs your yes.

What are the Niche Pack and the Launch Bundle?

Optional add-ons, paid once with your first month. The Niche Pack gives you 5 researched niches and 100 topics; pick one and your channel is set up for you, with its topics first in line. The Launch Bundle adds the Thumbnail & Title Pack: 40 title formulas and 6 thumbnail layouts your videos use automatically. Both get new material every month while your plan is active.

Will my videos get views or make money?

We can’t promise that, and nobody honest can. You get a well-made video on a topic you chose; growing a channel is still up to your topics, how often you post, and YouTube.
