    From channel idea to uploaded video. Every step is in the box.

    Plan.

    Find a niche that can win

    Checks a topic against six gates (demand, competition, earnings potential and more) before you commit. Or give it 1–3 channels you admire and it reverse-engineers their formula.

    → a go / no-go verdict with the evidence
    02

    Set up the channel

    Channel name, description, tags, and prompts for your profile picture and banner. Everything you need on day one.

    → a launch-ready channel profile
    03

    Pick the topic and the angle

    Finds what's rising in your lane right now, then lays out twenty angles on it so you make the version nobody has made yet.

    → the topic + an angle with room to win
    Phase 2

    Make.

    04

    Write a script that learns from your lane

    Reads the top videos in your niche and matches what works: hook length, number of beats, pacing. Copies of another creator's script are refused.

    → a script built on proof, not guesses
    05

    Build the visuals

    Writes the image prompts, renders them through your image service, pulls matching stock footage, and adds animated charts and captions.

    → every frame of the video
    06

    Voice and captions

    Natural narration from your voice service, with word-level timing so captions and pictures land exactly on the words.

    → voiceover + synced highlight captions
    Phase 3

    → a thumbnail made to get clicked
    08

    09
