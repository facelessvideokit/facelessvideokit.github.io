Refund policy

Faceless Video Kit · last updated 26 October 2026

The web app (app.facelessvideokit.com)

Full refund if you haven’t started a video. If no video has been started on your account since your payment, write to us and we refund that payment in full, and the credit it added is removed.

No refund once a video has used credit. Each video uses AI services that we pay for the moment it runs, so once any video has been started (credit held or used), plan payments and top-ups are not refundable.

Unused credit. Credit is not cash and is not paid out. Plan credit lasts one month from when it is added; top-up credit lasts 12 months. Whatever a video doesn’t use goes back to your wallet automatically.

A video that stops. If a video stops before it’s finished, you’re charged only for what it had already used, and the rest of what was held for it comes back to your wallet. If a fault on our side wasted your credit, write to us. A video left waiting for your answer closes itself after 6 days, and what was held for it comes back.

Cancelling. Cancel your plan any time from your Whop account. You keep your plan’s credit until it lapses; you won’t be charged again.

The downloadable Kit (Mac or PC)

Bought the downloadable Faceless Video Kit? Its refund window is 7 days from purchase.

How to ask

Email facelessvideokit.help@gmail.com from the email on your Whop account, or ask from your Whop account. Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Whop.
